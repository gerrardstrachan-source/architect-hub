# H2 v0.3 Adversarial Repair Record

The v0.2 H2 world contained a false equality claim for training action marginals. Under the actual contingencies, H-S yielded 128 A1 / 64 A0 while H-C yielded 96 A1 / 96 A0. This was identified before ACT-R prediction and blocked the immediate-performance gate.

The smallest state-set substitution found that preserves six training states and the matched observable framework is:

- remove training states `010` and `101`;
- add training states `001` and `100`;
- retain 32 repetitions per training state and 192 training trials.

The resulting active training set is `000, 001, 011, 100, 110, 111`. Both H-S and H-C then have 2 A0 states and 4 A1 states, yielding exactly 64 A0 and 128 A1 trials in each history.

All three feature marginals are also balanced at 96 zeros / 96 ones. A now withholds `010` and `101`, which are the only joint states absent from training and first appear in A.

The repair was independently validated from the local complete fixture. No ACT-R parameter adjustment or post-hoc model compensation was used.

An attempted repository CSV transfer was recognized as header-only and is not evidence. The canonical committed fixture representation is the complete state-sequence artifact.

No ACT-R prediction, implementation, fixture freeze, or H2 execution is authorized.
